About Dose of Clarity

Dose of Clarity is a student-built guide to how common medicines work. It is written by Dhruv Karai, a BSc Pharmacology student, to make medicine information easier to understand.

How it is written

Each page is based on UK sources: the NHS website, the British National Formulary (BNF) and electronic medicines compendium (eMC) product information. Every page lists its sources and the date they were checked.

What it is not

It is not medical advice and it shows typical adult doses only and does not give personal doses. Everyone is different, so speak to a pharmacist or doctor about your own medicines. In an emergency call 999.
